22 Best Apple Dessert Recipes for Fall

When apple season arrives, it’s time to turn crispy, juicy apples into fantastic desserts. So, we’ve gathered 22 of the best apple dessert recipes for fall, filled with sugar, spice, and everything nice. What you’ll find is classic apple pies, easy apple crisps and crumbles, caramel apple desserts, apple dumplings and turnovers, quick dump cakes, apple cakes and coffee cakes that are perfect for fall.

DONNA BARDOCZ
HOWELL, MI

Apple Slab Pie

“This is a delicious recipe with a crust that is flaky and rich. Cream cheese and butter are a match made in heaven for a wonderful tasting crust. I love this slab pie because not only does it taste wonderful, but it feeds a lot of people. It is great to make for a pot luck or for a family gathering. It never fails to impress and gets rave reviews.” – Donna
Video Paused
CATHY HURKMANS TOLMAN
ESCANABA, MI

Caramel Glazed Soft Apple Pie

“My hubby loves apple pie but prefers softer apples and not tart. Over the years, I’ve practiced & this is what I finally decided is the final choice. He loved it.” – Cathy
LAURA DAVIS
RUSK, TX

Apple Dumplings

“Serve this with vanilla ice cream. This recipe could very easily be made for a diabetic, using Diet Sprite and Splenda.” – Laura
STEVEN WILSON
CHESTERFIELD, VA

Classic Apple Pie

“The lemon zest and fresh lemon juice in this pie make it taste so fresh and even more delicious!” – Steven
MAGGIE HEDEN
ANTIOCH, IL

Apple Crumble Bars

“These are as easy as it gets! Also great with any kind of fruit filling.” – Maggie
JEWEL HALL
CULLMAN, AL

Apple Crisp Best Ever! No Oats

“I never cared for oats on my Apple Betty or apple crisp. This is delicious! For a variety, I add some chopped walnuts or pecans and substitute the apple juice with orange juice. It’s apple picking time!” – Jewel
BETSY WOLFE
FT WORTH, TX

Apple Enchiladas

“These apple enchiladas are a great fall or winter dessert. Easier than pie or cake, but smells great in the oven. Made with a few ingredients and ready to eat in minutes. This goes well after many meals or serve it with breakfast.” – Betsy
LISA GLASS
ENGLEWOOD, OH

Kentucky Apple Cake

“When my mother passed away, a close family friend made us two fantastic cakes. This apple cake is one of them! Both my father and my boyfriend couldn’t stop raving about this one and said they could eat this cake all day, every day.” – Lisa
SHAWNA PAYNE
LILBURN, GA

Poor Man’s Apple Cobbler

“This is the easiest cobbler you can make in a pinch. If you have unexpected company and need a quick and easy dessert, give this a try.” – Shawna
ANGELA (GRAMMY) DERBY
CHARLOTTE, NC

Apple Pie Filling Coffee Cake

“Who doesn’t like a nice piece of coffee cake for breakfast, or anytime, for that matter? Especially for overnight company… it’s just a real treat. So, instead of running to the grocery store and buying donuts or coffee cake, just make this one up in no time. Your guests and family will love you even more for it. Mine did!” – Angela (Grammy)
CHELSEA MITCHAM
HOUSTON, TX

Double Caramel Apple Cookies

“I adapted these from several different recipes and was so proud of how they came out! Homemade caramel can be substituted by store-bought, but I love knowing these are completely from scratch.” – Chelsea
CRYSTAL VILLA
GOODYEAR, AZ

Blue Ribbon Apple Bundt Cake

“As the name implies, this cake won first place at my state fair! This cake always seems to be a hit with everyone that tries it. This cake is also excellent without glaze; instead, I serve it slightly warm with vanilla ice cream.” – Crystal
FEATURED BLOGGER

Cinnamon Apple Crisp

“This cinnamon apple crisp is full of warm, juicy apples and topped with a buttery oat crunch. Topped with ice cream, the perfect dessert.” – Debbie
TERESA JACOBSON
VIRGINIA, MN

Apple Cranberry Galette

“A galette is basically a free-formed one-crust pie. It can be a dessert, main dish, or appetizer. When you think of it that way, anything can be put into a galette! This is one that I came up with when I was thinking of making fried apples for dinner.” – Teresa
BRENDA SCORE
LAKE ELMO, MN

Apple Bundt with Brown-Butter Vanilla Bean Glaze

“Apple cake perfection, full of moist apple goodness and a thick buttery glaze.” – Brenda
CAROL JUNKINS
HANSON, MA

Swedish Apple Pie

“The easiest Swedish apple pie you will ever make. Great if you have extra apples and want to use them up quickly.” – Carol
DEB BALDWIN
SAINT MARIES, ID

Apple Turnovers

“I like to make turnovers. I just made 4 apple turnovers and some cups filled with the filling and a topping using my apple cobbler topping. This will make a nice snack for after dinner.” – Deb
LAURA DASZYNICZ
UNION CITY, PA

Deep Dish Apple Crisp

“I made this recipe by trial and error. I love apple pie, but sometimes do not have time to make a crust, so I came up with this. The recipe is easy and quick to make. Served warm with ice cream, it makes a nice company dessert.” – Laura
RHONDA WHEELER
WARREN, MI

Apple Rhubarb Crisp

“Easy summer dessert and it tastes great too. My grandma gave me this recipe.” – Rhonda
DAVID BRANDON
CLAUDVILLE, VA

Apple Streusel Coffee Cake

“This cake is a sweet, crunchy treat to bake at apple-picking time! We used Winesap apples, and it turned out great.” – David
JANE PATRICK
OCALA, FL

Caramel Pecan Apple Dump Cake

“I came up with this recipe to take to our family get together at Christmas this year. Feel free to tweak it anyway you want.” – Jane
FEATURED BLOGGER

Caramel Apple Crumble Cheesecake Dip

“Caramel apple crumble served on top of a cheesecake layer as a dip!” – Kevin
