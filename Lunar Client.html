Lunar Client x PvPHQ

Written by Physci
Listen to audio version of this article

Two of the biggest names in competitive Minecraft PvP have joined forces. FlowPvP and MCLeagues have merged to create PvPHQ, a brand-new competitive PvP server backed by Lunar Client. Built from the ground up for competitive play, PvPHQ aims to deliver the best PvP experience the community has ever seen. Join the PvPHQ Discord and follow us on Twitter to stay up to date.

What is PvPHQ?

PvPHQ is a new competitive Minecraft PvP network created through the merger of FlowPvP and MCLeagues. Over the past month, the combined team has been working closely with developers, top players, and creators to bring the best parts of both projects together and build something entirely new. Lunar Client is proud to support and back the project.

Built on Lunar Client

PvPHQ is being built from the ground up alongside Lunar Client to deliver a fully integrated competitive experience. Players will be able to queue directly through Lunar Client and benefit from client-side optimizations and features designed specifically for PvPHQ. Lunar’s involvement goes far beyond just the client as well, with anti-cheat support, exclusive cosmetics, creator activations, tournaments, and partnerships backed by 8 years of experience in the Minecraft community.

PvP That Actually Feels Fair

PvPHQ has built something that’s never existed on a Minecraft server before: a system designed to make fights feel as if everyone is playing on the same low latency, even when they aren’t. No more losing fights just because your opponent has a better connection. Ender pearls, wind charges, hit registration, anti-ghosting, you name it. Everything feels cleaner, smoother, and more consistent. If you’ve ever been frustrated by how much latency impacts PvP, this is the solution.

22 Locations Worldwide

PvPHQ is launching with 22 server locations across North America, Europe, Asia, the Middle East, Oceania, and more. A smart queue system helps ensure you’re always getting into matches quickly and on a fair connection. Fast queues, fair matches.

A Ranking System That Actually Means Something

PvPHQ’s ranked mode is designed to reflect actual skill, not just who can grind the most games. Your rank will adjust based on the quality of your wins and losses, creating a more competitive and accurate leaderboard experience. More details can be found in the PvPHQ Discord.

Big Events, Big Prizes

With the full teams behind FlowPvP and MCLeagues working together, PvPHQ will be hosting large-scale tournaments and live events with serious prize pools. More details on that coming soon.

Packed With Features

PvPHQ is setting a new standard for PvP servers from day one with the number of features launching alongside it. Custom kits, ranked map selection, a replay system, party team fights, advanced kit customization, tournaments, social profiles, post-match analysis, custom shields and trims, totem anti-ghost, and performance optimizations are all built in from the start. PvPHQ is launching with more features than most servers add over the course of years.

Cheaters Aren't Welcome Here

PvPHQ is taking anti-cheat seriously. HWID detection combined with Lunar Client’s client-side integration makes alting and ban evasion much harder to pull off. This is only the beginning too, with even more improvements already in the works.

What's Next?

There’s still plenty more to come. Follow PvPHQ and stay tuned for sneak peeks, announcements, and everything leading up to launch.
