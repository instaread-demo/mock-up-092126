Digital Tamers 2 – Anniversary Update (V2.0.0) Now Live!

Published 2 weeks ago by Raextor , updated 2 weeks ago
Listen to audio version of this article
Sponsored by
0:00 / 10:22
Instaread

Two years to the day after Digital Tamers 2 first launched, developer DragonRod has dropped the game's biggest update yet. Version 2.0.0 has now been live on PC and Android for a couple of days, and if you haven't grabbed it yet, head over to DragonRod's official itch.io page right now.

This is not a small anniversary bundle. V2.0.0 introduces an entirely new game mode, more than 60 new equipment items (over 100 new items in total), an in-game Quest Log, a way to flip day and night without waiting on the real-world clock, a pile of quality-of-life changes, a massive Leader Skill rebalance, the first official (partial) Portuguese translation, and 27 new Digimon. Among them are beloved Digimon from the Liberator web series that the community has been buzzing about since the Cats & Dogs update.

But first things first. Did you notice that we just jumped from 1.2.1 to 2.0.0?

Versioning

Back in the Cats & Dogs article, I covered DragonRod's move to proper semantic versioning. Recap, because it finally matters:

  • Major (X): big, structural changes, for example, new systems that change how the game is played.
  • Minor (Y): new content that stays backward-compatible.
  • Patch (Z): fixes and small tweaks.

Until now, every DT2 update lived in the 1.x range. Adventure Mode, portable Leader Cartridges, and a Quest Log are exactly the kind of "this changes how you play" work that a major version is supposed to mark. Two years in, Digital Tamers 2 has officially entered 2.x.

Adventure Mode

The headline feature is the Adventure Mode, a roguelike challenge where you build up Rental Partners and try to fight through 100 enemies.

The loop looks like this: you pick a partner from a pool of 77 possible Rookies, fight a battle, then choose one perk from a set of four before the next fight. Every 10 battles, you get a step reward and the option to either keep going or pause the run and come back later. Rinse and repeat until you clear all 100.

At the end of a successful run, you pick one of two rewards:

  • Keep the Rental Digimon. It can retain up to 60% of the Training stats it earned during that Adventure, which makes a rental partner stronger than a regular partner with raw training could achieve.
  • Take a Box of Leader Cartridges. These are portable versions of Leader Skills that go in a Digimon's Equipment slot, freeing up one of the three possible leader skill slots, potentially allowing you to stack 4. This opens up a ton of new and overpowered builds for your favorite Digimon.

To unlock the mode, you need to:

  • Finish Part 1 of A Good Friend.
  • Have at least 3 Digimon recruited to Server City.
  • Complete a short quest featuring Ropuremon.

New Features

On top of the new mode, V2.0.0 adds several systems, features, and additions the game has needed for a while:

  • An in-game Quest Log, opened from the DigiVice menu.
  • 60+ new Equipment items (100 new items in total)
  • A way to cycle Day/Night from inside the game: recruit Clockmon to Server City, and he can turn day into night and night into day. If you can only play at certain hours of the day, you are no longer locked out of time-of-day content.
  • Partial Portuguese translation, toggleable in the settings menu. DragonRod made it clear that this is not finished yet! Some menus and items still display in English and will do so for a while.

On that last point: Grindosaur will only start featuring the Portuguese localization after it has been finished and fully implemented.

Gameplay Changes

A lot of early-game friction got sanded down, and a few older systems were rebuilt:

  • Hina now gifts the player some Hunting Gear during the tutorial.
  • Some early NPC dialogue has been shortened.
  • A new NPC was added in Mecha Plants during A Good Friend – Part 1 to fix a line that used to send people the wrong way.
  • Portable Potty now activates instantly when there is poop on the floor.
  • The Supplemon quest now starts at 6,000 total kills (down from 10,000)
  • The Nanimon quest now starts at 3,500 total kills (down from 5,000)
  • Wandering Merchants make a noise when they spawn.
  • Glitches use a different sound effect when you re-enter a map.

Three of the bigger systemic changes deserve their own callouts.

Datamon no longer resets your Tamer Points. Instead of wiping progress, Datamon now increases the player's maximum level, which allows you to buy all Tamer Skills again, finally! No more Tamer Skill resets!

The Collab Quest reward has been replaced. If you already finished that quest, go back to the meeting point and trigger the final dialogue again to collect the new reward. After you beat Rurimon in Server City, it will also start showing up as a Dungeon Master in Green Domain.

Infinity Tower's exclusive rewards have been moved into Adventure Mode. The Tower still exists, but it now pays Bits every 10 waves instead of the old exclusive-item track. That mode is more or less dead in its current form; DragonRod has a rework planned for later.

Buffs

Leader Skills were the main balance pass this time, and the numbers are not small:

  • Skills that "Greatly increase" stats now boost them from 15% (capping at 1,500) to 35% (capping at 5,500)
  • Skills that "Increase" stats now boost them from 8% (capping at 800) to 15% (capping at 2,750)
  • Damage-dealing Leader Skills now use the user's level as minimum damage instead of a flat 8
  • Blasting Flame base damage increased.
  • Leader Skills affecting the field saw a Defense boost buff from 6% to 25%, and now affect both Defense and Special Defense
  • Direction saw a Special Attack boost from 6% to 20%, and now affects both Attack and Special Attack
  • Imposing Posture saw the opponent's Attack and Special Attack drop from 2% to 15%
  • Special Charge I saw its Ultimate Gauge boost increase from 15% to 35%
  • Sacrifice's stat boost has been increased from 12% to 45%
  • Sacrifice II's stat boost has been increased from 15% to 65%
  • Frenzy's stat boost has been increased from 2% to 20%, and it now maximizes critical hit ratio
  • Sand Surprise: each Ganimon hit now drops the opponent's Sp. Atk by the user's level
  • Targeted Surge damage reduction increased from 1/5 to 1/4
  • Critical hit odds increased for Crit builds
  • All dual-melee Digimon: S1 base damage increased

Technical Changes

DragonRod is setting the game up for limited-time events (Halloween, Christmas, and so on) without having to ship a whole new build to flip a calendar flag. To support that, V2.0.0 locks the game if the system clock is pushed forward to skip ahead in-game.

For now, setting the clock back to the real date and tapping the middle of the screen lets you keep playing. DragonRod has said that in a future version this will fully lock the affected save.

If you hit that lock screen without changing your system clock, do not tap through it first. Take a screenshot of the on-screen info, post it in the Bug Report channel of the official Digital Tamers 2 Discord, and then tap to continue. That report is how they want to catch false positives before the stricter lock goes live.

Bug Fixes

The official fix list for 2.0.0 is short:

  • Imperialdramon Paladin Mode's ability now activates correctly with melee attacks.

Known Bugs

A few issues shipped with the anniversary build. Workarounds, where they exist:

  • Sangomon's recruiting quest may show up blank in the Quest Log. Talk to him once in Server City to refresh it.
  • PicoDevimon is appearing during the day in Log-In Village. DragonRod says this will be fixed in 2.0.1, releasing next week.
  • If you beat the Gaioumon Itto Mode quest before 2.0.0, it will not count as completed. You will need to beat it again. This is because the game lacked an additional variable to track kills via a quest flag or a kill counter.

New Digimon

And the part most of you scrolled here for. V2.0.0 adds 27 Digimon. The roster is a mix of Liberator newcomers, palette swaps, and a few long-requested names:

  • Cendrillmon
  • Chaperomon
  • DeckerGreymon
  • DORUgoramon
  • Doumon
  • ElDradimon
  • Fluffymon
  • Galemon
  • GrandGalemon
  • Kyaromon
  • Kyubimon (Silver)
  • MedievalDukemon
  • Nyabootmon
  • Ohakadamon
  • Otamamon (Red)
  • Pafumon
  • Pteromon
  • Shoemon
  • ShoeShoemon
  • Shoutmon X3
  • Stingmon (Black)
  • Taomon (Silver)
  • Tuchidarumon
  • XV-mon (Black)
  • Yolkmon
  • Youkomon
  • Zephagamon

If that Liberator set looks familiar, it should. After Cats & Dogs, and before the anniversary patch was supposed to launch, a smaller Liberator web-novel update was planned as its own drop. The workload behind Adventure Mode swallowed that plan, and the two were merged into the anniversary update instead.

Final Thoughts

V2.0.0, similar to the big story updates, is one of those Digital Tamers 2 updates that really plays like a new chapter instead of "more Digimon plus a dungeon." The Adventure Mode offers repeatable long-term progression that forces you to try out new Digimon you would have never picked otherwise and differs strongly from Infinity Tower, a game mode that offered little in regards to engaging gameplay.

Leader Cartridges and the equipment dump change how you kit partners, and the Datamon rewrite removes one of the more punishing account-level systems in the game.

The clock-lock change is the one to read twice before you mess with your device's date. It is there for future events; it is not a full save brick yet, and the Discord report path exists specifically for people who get flagged by mistake.

Two years in, DT2 is still a one-developer fan game that keeps expanding instead of winding down. That is rare. Grab the build from itch.io, recruit Clockmon if you hate waiting for night, and take Ropuremon's quest seriously if you want to get into Adventure Mode.

As always, stay tuned. I'll keep you posted as soon as we learn more.
