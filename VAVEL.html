Real Sociedad Leave it Late at Mestalla

Action late on meant that Real Sociedad would snatch all three points at the Mestella against a struggling Valencia side.

By Lenny MorrisUpdated September 20, 2026, 11:55 pm BST

Valencia looked set to claw themselves a point at the Mestalla after what has been a turbulent season so far for the hosts, yet it was not to be with Ander Barrenetxea scoring late on to give Real Sociedad a much needed win to push up the LaLiga table.

The hosts Valencia came into this match as the side that sat second bottom.

After their 1-0 defeat away at the Ramón Sánchez-Pizjuán Stadium to a strong Sevilla side that has exceeded expectations so far this season, Carlos Corberán was dismissed from the club. This was alongside the club's chief executive, with the club languishing at the foot of the table with one point, which came in a goalless draw at home to Celta Vigo.

That being said, since their defeat in the capital of Andalucia, the Blanquinegros pushed themselves up the table with a 1-0 away win to Quique Sánchez Flores’ Deportivo Alaves, with substitute Luis Rioja getting the winner before he was replaced with an injury.

As for the visitors, they are the current Copa del Rey holders but have started the season poorly.

In their opening six matches of the season, the side based in San Sebastian had seven points, with two wins, one draw and three losses, but would have seen the match against a poor Valencia side as a match in which they could kick-start their season.

Sucic The Difference At The Break

Real Sociedad went into the break 1-0 up, and it was a scoreline that had reflected the opening 45 minutes.

In the opening minutes, Luken Beitia had the first effort of the match, when his strike proved unproblematic for Valencia’s goalkeeper Stole Dimitrievski.

Minutes later, Job Ochieng had an effort which was taken on his right foot after he was played in by World Cup winner Mikel Oyarzabal, though his effort was blocked.

Luken Beitia would have another effort on goal when the centre-back connected with a cross from Sergio Gómez with a cross just minutes prior to an effort from outside the box by Carlos Soler.

Though Txuri-urdin would eventually find their breakthrough in the 26th minute, when Luka Sucic would rifle his first-time effort into the top left-hand corner after receiving a back-heel pass from 23-year-old Ochieng when he received the ball under pressure on the edge of the six-yard box.

From here, the hosts looked to get back into the game, but Real Sociedad were able to see the match out for the remainder of the first half.

Late Action Gives The Visitors All Three Points

After their poor start to the season, the next goal was pivotal for Valencia as they would have hoped to rewrite their season in their second match under interim manager Oscar Sanchez.

Nerves were creeping in around the Mestalla, but those fears would soon be reduced when Valencia got an equaliser with a stunning strike from Aaron Mayol.

After a cross floated into the box with striker Hugo Duro missing the header, the ball deflected off his head and landed on the edge of the box, where Mayol smashed it home a first-time half-volley into the top right-hand corner, which proved too quick and too powerful for Alex Remiro to prevent.

That being said, after what turned into an end-to-end match, Real Sociedad would be awarded a golden opportunity from the spot, with former West Ham loanee Carlos Soler placing the ball into the bottom left-hand corner, with enough precision to beat Dimitrievski, who guessed the right way.

He wasn't the last player in a Sociedad shirt to find the back of the net, with Luke Beitia converting a cross into his own goal in the 83rd minute, which slowly crept past Remiro in the Sociedad goal.

Yet, as all hope seemed lost, Real Sociedad would win a corner in stoppage time, which they made count.

The corner was played short, with Barrenetxea receiving the pass. After a dummy to wrong foot of the Valencia defender, he played a pass to Solder, who threaded a through ball which split the hosts' defence, with the striker, who started on the bench, taking a touch and smashing the ball past Dimitrievski at the near post, with the ball finishing in the roof of the net.

The action wasn't finished with Icelandic striker Orri Óskarsson given his marching orders in the dying embers of the match, but moments after, the match concluded, and Real Sociedad bounced back from their defeat in the UEFA Europa League to AFC Bournemouth.
